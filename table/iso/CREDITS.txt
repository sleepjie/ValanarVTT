The sheets were re-encoded as WebP and the props cropped and packed into one sheet; the dungeon sheets join
rubberduck's wall and floor sheets with m7600's floors. Apart from what is noted above, the art is unchanged.
Under CC-BY-SA, these sheets (and changes to them) stay CC-BY-SA.
The tile layout rules (banks, cliffs, cave walls) were learned from Flare's own maps (flare-game, CC-BY-SA 3.0).
